  [fk_to_cloud] FKs from LokaDB tables to cloud tables: for a moved organization, whose rows are in its own database, lokadb's triggers stand in for them. A row written through the cloud must reference an existing cloud row, and deleting or changing the key of a cloud row applies the FK's action (CASCADE, SET NULL, RESTRICT) in the moved organizations' databases too (see fk_to_cloud_delete). There they are checked at the end of each statement, even if DEFERRABLE, and writes that do not go through the cloud database (made in the organization's database itself) are not checked

Warnings (1)
  [fk_to_cloud_delete] accounts is referenced by LokaDB tables (conversations.account_id -> accounts; messages.account_id -> accounts) and does not tell the organization of its rows (it is not in routing.cloud_tables): once an organization is moved, deleting a row of accounts or changing its key has to check or change the rows referencing it in every moved organization's database, as a statement on LokaDB tables of no one organization does. With the fanout policy is reject (default, no -proxy-config) lokadb refuses such a DELETE or UPDATE as soon as one organization is moved, the rows referencing it or not. To keep it within one organization's database, declare accounts in routing.cloud_tables with the column that holds the organization of its rows, or set the fanout policy of the database to log or allow
